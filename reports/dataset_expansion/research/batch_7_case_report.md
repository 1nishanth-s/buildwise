## 2. Critical Compatibility Clearances & Data Hygiene

Each candidate has verified:
1. **Motherboard Form Factor Support:** Comprehensive list mapping exact compatible standards (`Mini-ITX`, `Micro-ATX`, `ATX`, `E-ATX`, `SSI-EEB`).
2. **GPU Clearance:** Exact maximum length in mm (ranging from 320mm in compact SFF up to 549mm in workstation towers).
3. **CPU Air Cooler Clearance:** Maximum vertical height in mm (ranging from 53mm in sandwich SFF up to 205mm in massive towers).
4. **PSU Support & Length:** SFX vs ATX format and maximum PSU length in mm.
5. **Liquid Cooling Radiator Topography:** Full listing of radiator mounting rails (120mm, 240mm, 280mm, 360mm, 420mm, 480mm).
6. **I/O Modernization:** Recording USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C ports across all modern models.

Zero collision with production catalog was verified.
