System Architecture & Philosophy

About BuildWise-AI

An open, evidence-based PC recommendation and compatibility validation platform designed specifically for transparent hardware planning.

Deterministic Compatibility

Unlike generic AI chat models that hallucinate non-existent hardware combinations (such as DDR4 RAM on DDR5-only AM5 motherboards), BuildWise-AI enforces a deterministic rule engine:

  • Socket & Pin Matching (LGA1700, LGA1851, AM4, AM5).
  • Memory Architecture Validation (DIMM DDR4 vs DDR5).
  • TDP and Continuous Power Supply headroom margins (≥25%).
  • Motherboard Form Factor vs Case Chamber Clearance.
  • M.2 NVMe Key and PCIe generation lane allocation.

Verified Indian Retail Pricing

Component recommendations reflect genuine, live-verified retail price observations from authorized Indian hardware vendors:

  • Direct storefront verification: MD Computers, Prime ABGB, Vedant Computers.
  • Zero fabricated prices or currency conversions from international MSRPs.
  • Audited against manufacturer part numbers (MPN/SKU).
  • Clear timestamps and in-stock inventory confirmation.

Three-State Transparency

We strictly separate proven compatibility from missing information:

  • Compatible (Green): Formally verified by manufacturer specifications.
  • Unknown (Amber): Data is unverified (e.g., custom cooler height vs cabinet clearance); human measurement recommended.
  • Incompatible (Red): Proven physical, thermal, or electrical violation.
